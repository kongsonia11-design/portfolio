Brief:
Design homeware and furniture that spark interaction and connection, focusing on provenance and material exploration through denim.


![](/images/projects/muti-functionalbutton2.png)

## Design Conncept
Denim, often bleached, dyed, and overlooked, is reimagined as upholstery. Shaping and tucking the material creates a continuous, flowing surface that reflects themes of fast fashion waste and nature.
