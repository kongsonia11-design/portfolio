Sustainability Goal: Life on Land

A user-centred, interactive cabinet designed to raise awareness of biodiversity and sustainability. The piece tells a story through touch, engaging users with natural materials to create an emotional connection and encourage reflection on the protection of ecosystems for future generations. This project highlights material-led design, sensory interaction, and educational storytelling.

![](/images/projects/cabinet2.jpg)

## Design Concept 

Inspired by the sensory qualities of seed cases, the design reveals the hidden beauty of everyday natural structures through tactility, subtle pattern, and interaction.

![](/images/projects/cabinet4.jpg)

## Day and Night

The contrast between the transparent resin door and the solid wooden door highlights the interplay of light and darkness, reflecting the changing nature of life.
