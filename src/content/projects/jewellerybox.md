## Notice the newly grown flowers and plants around you?

Blossom captures the essence of seasonal change and translates it into a functional jewellery and storage box designed for modern living spaces. Inspired by nature in bloom, the design reflects growth, softness, and organic beauty through form and colour.

Created for design-conscious users, Blossom offers a practical solution for organising small, easily misplaced items. It helps declutter everyday surfaces while providing a dedicated and accessible storage system tailored for jewellery and other small essentials.

Made from Jesmonite and mountboard, the design combines durability with precise construction. It features a smooth sliding drawer mechanism with multiple compartments, allowing flexible organisation for different user needs while maintaining a clean, sculptural appearance.
