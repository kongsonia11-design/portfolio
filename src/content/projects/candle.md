Discarded cans are given new life!

Through sandblasting, an expressive pattern of creases is formed, then a hole is extruded to hold a candlestick. A simple transformation that turns waste into meaningful and functional design.

![](/images/projects/candleshoot-1.png)

![](/images/projects/candleshoot-2.png)

![](/images/projects/candleshoot-3.png)

## Design Concept

Creases that echo the fleeting nature of time. As wax drips down, it adds beauty — reminding us of impermanence, while giving new life to what was once waste.

![](/images/projects/candleshoot-4.png)

Natural wax dripping effect creates a visually pleasing, organic look, adding character and charm to the upcycled can candlestick holder.
